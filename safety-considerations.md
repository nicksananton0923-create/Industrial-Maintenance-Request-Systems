# Safety Considerations — Industrial Electrical Maintenance and Breakdown Analysis

## Lockout-Tagout (LOTO)

LOTO is mandatory for any electrical work. The site's LOTO procedure must be followed strictly.

## Live Working

Live working is generally prohibited. Work on energised equipment requires:

- A specific permit
- Specialised PPE (arc-rated suit, insulated gloves, face shield, arc-rated hearing protection)
- A second person trained in CPR and first aid
- Verification that the work cannot be done de-energised

## Arc Flash Hazard

Arc flash analysis should be performed for switchgear above 240 V (per IEEE 1584). The analysis determines:

- The incident energy at the working distance
- The arc flash protection boundary
- The required PPE category

The analysis results must be displayed on the switchgear's warning label.

## Insulation Testing (Megger)

Insulation testing requires:

- The equipment to be isolated (LOTO applied)
- The equipment to be discharged before and after the test
- The test voltage to be appropriate for the equipment's rated voltage
- Test results to be trended over time

## Thermographic Inspection

Thermographic inspection is typically performed under load (energised):

- Use a qualified thermographer.
- Wear appropriate PPE (arc-rated clothing, face shield).
- Maintain safe working distance per the arc flash analysis.
- Do not open energised panel doors beyond the arc flash boundary.

## Earth Fault Loop Impedance Testing

Earth fault loop impedance testing requires the equipment to be energised:

- Follow the test instrument manufacturer's safety instructions.
- Verify the equipment is suitable for live testing before proceeding.

## Confined Space Entry

Transformer inspection may require confined space entry:

- Follow the site's CSE procedure.
- Atmospheric monitoring, attendant, rescue plan, and entry permit are mandatory.

## Disclaimer

This framework is documentation only. All electrical work must be performed by a qualified electrician.
